SYSTEMDECODED

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

SystemDecoded is a technology content and automation platform used to create, manage, publish, and analyse educational technology content. This Privacy Policy explains how SystemDecoded accesses, uses, stores, and protects information, including information obtained through Google and YouTube APIs.

1. Information We Access

When a user connects a Google or YouTube account to SystemDecoded using Google OAuth, the application may access information permitted by the scopes approved by the user.

This may include:

  • Basic Google account identity information such as email address.
  • YouTube channel identity and channel metadata.
  • Metadata about videos belonging to the connected YouTube channel.
  • YouTube Analytics data for the connected channel and its videos.

SystemDecoded does not request access to the user's Google account password.

2. Google and YouTube OAuth Permissions

SystemDecoded currently uses read-only Google and YouTube permissions to identify the connected account, verify channel and video ownership, retrieve video metadata, and retrieve YouTube Analytics reports.

The application currently does not automatically publish or upload videos to YouTube through the YouTube API. Publishing remains a user-controlled manual workflow.

3. How We Use Google User Data

Google and YouTube data accessed by SystemDecoded is used only to provide application features such as:

  • Connecting and identifying the user's YouTube channel.
  • Verifying ownership of published videos.
  • Displaying channel and video metadata.
  • Retrieving daily YouTube Analytics data.
  • Retrieving lifecycle performance metrics such as D1, D3, D7, D28, and D90 analytics.
  • Displaying content-performance information inside the SystemDecoded analytics and content-intelligence interfaces.

Google user data is not used for advertising, sold to third parties, or used for unrelated purposes.

4. Data Storage

SystemDecoded stores application data necessary to operate its features. This may include YouTube channel identifiers, video identifiers, metadata, analytics results, publication records, and content performance information.

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely and encrypted at rest. Access tokens are refreshed when necessary to maintain an authorised connection.

5. Data Sharing

SystemDecoded does not sell Google user data.

Google and YouTube user data is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the requested application functionality, comply with applicable law, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

6. Data Retention

SystemDecoded retains connected account metadata, analytics, and related application records only for as long as they are needed to provide the service, maintain historical analytics, or meet legitimate operational requirements.

Users may disconnect their YouTube account from SystemDecoded. The application will remove stored OAuth credentials associated with that connection and will no longer retrieve new Google or YouTube data.

7. Revoking Google Account Access

Users may revoke SystemDecoded's access to their Google account at any time through their Google Account permissions page.

Revoking access prevents SystemDecoded from making further authorised Google or YouTube API requests until the account is connected again.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

SystemDecoded's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Security

SystemDecoded uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect stored information, including encrypted OAuth credentials, access controls, and application-level validation.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable safeguards are maintained to reduce unauthorised access or misuse.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if SystemDecoded's features, data practices, or Google API usage change.

Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.
